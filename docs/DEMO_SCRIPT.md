Judging criteria to hit: real-world impact, theme, works end to end, use of AI, creativity, originality, demo quality.
Lead with the red-flag look-through: it's the part no other retail tool does. Overlap and fees are supporting evidence.

| Time | Screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Landing page | "Your mutual funds own companies you've never looked at. Some of them have warning signs sitting in their own annual reports. Nobody reads 300-page reports, so nobody tells you. Pardarshi does." |
| 0:25–0:45 | *Start with whatever you have* | "Upload a CAS, pick funds by name, or paste a list. The PDF is read in memory and never saved; your PAN never reaches the AI." Click **Try a sample portfolio**. |
| 0:45–1:50 | Report → COL. 01 warning signs → **See evidence** → **Open page** | "We looked through these six funds to the companies inside and read their annual reports. This company: profit rose but operating cash fell to a third of profit. Here's the exact line on page 7, machine-verified. And you hold it through these two funds." Show *Rule* and *How it was read*. |
| 1:50–2:30 | **Before the fall** page | "Does this work? We ran the same checks on reports filed *before* a company's collapse. N of 7 warning signs were already there, each with its page." |
| 2:30–3:05 | COL. 02 and 03 | "We also add up what you really own: these six funds behave like under two. Their regular plans cost about ₹7 lakh over 20 years." Type age 25 to recalculate. |
| 3:05–3:35 | **Ask a question** → "Why is this company flagged?" | "Ask in English or Hindi. Answers cite pages; if the AI is down you still get the verified findings." |
| 3:35–4:05 | Beginner/Expert switch, a tappable term, Hindi toggle, glossary | "Built for first-time investors and experts: plain words, every term explained, every formula one tap away." |
| 4:05–4:40 | Methodology page → Measured accuracy; terminal running `pytest` | "On X hand-checked cases our flags had Y% precision and Z% recall; 100% of quotes are verified on their page. 50 automated tests." |
| 4:40–5:00 | Landing page | "Pardarshi: transparent by name, transparent by design. Not investment advice, just the truth about what you own." |

Tips: record at 1280×800, zoom the browser to 110%, and say clearly which data is real and which is the fictional demo.
